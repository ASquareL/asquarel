# Complete Crypto & Blockchain Course - ASquareL Academy ✅

## 🎓 Course Summary

A comprehensive 6-module, 30+ lesson course covering all aspects of blockchain technology, cryptocurrency, smart contracts, and decentralized finance.

---

## 📚 Complete Curriculum

### **Module 1: Blockchain Fundamentals** ✅
*5 Lessons | 45-60 minutes each*

- **Lesson 1.1:** Blockchain Basics & History
  - Bitcoin's invention, digital currency evolution
  - Core blockchain principles and architecture
  - Why decentralization matters

- **Lesson 1.2:** Distributed Ledgers & Consensus
  - Distributed ledger technology
  - Proof of Work (PoW) vs Proof of Stake (PoS)
  - Other consensus mechanisms

- **Lesson 1.3:** Cryptography & Security
  - Hash functions and their properties
  
  - Public-key cryptography
  - Digital signatures and verification
  - Merkle trees

- **Lesson 1.4:** Blocks, Transactions & Mining
  - Block structure and components
  - Transaction anatomy (UTXO vs Account models)
  - Mining process and difficulty adjustment
  - Mining rewards and incentives

- **Lesson 1.5:** Project - Blockchain Explorer
  - Using Etherscan and Bitcoin explorers
  - Analyzing real transactions
  - Understanding gas and fees
  - Reading smart contract data

---

### **Module 2: Cryptocurrency Ecosystems** ✅
*5 Lessons | 50-90 minutes each*

- **Lesson 2.1:** Wallets & Key Management
  - Hot wallets vs Cold wallets
  - Seed phrases and recovery
  - Private key, public key, and address hierarchy
  - Hardware wallet setup and security

- **Lesson 2.2:** Crypto Exchanges
  - Centralized Exchanges (CEX) - Coinbase, Kraken, Binance
  - Decentralized Exchanges (DEX) - Uniswap, PancakeSwap
  - Trading pairs and order types
  - Exchange security and best practices

- **Lesson 2.3:** Trading Fundamentals
  - Bid/Ask spreads and liquidity
  - Long and short positions
  - Order types (market, limit, stop-loss)
  - Risk management and position sizing
  - Leverage trading (advanced)

- **Lesson 2.4:** Market Analysis
  - Technical analysis basics
  - Candlestick charts and timeframes
  - Key indicators (MA, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands)
  - Support and resistance levels
  - Fundamental analysis for crypto

- **Lesson 2.5:** Project - Portfolio Management
  - Creating a diversified portfolio
  - Cost basis tracking
  - Performance calculation
  - Risk metrics (volatility, drawdown, Sharpe ratio)
  - Tax implications

---

### **Module 3: Smart Contracts with Solidity** ✅
*5 Lessons | 50-70 minutes each*

- **Lesson 3.1:** Introduction to Smart Contracts
  - What are smart contracts and why they matter
  - Solidity language overview
  - Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM)
  - Gas and transaction costs
  - Security considerations
  - Development tools (Remix, Hardhat, Truffle)

- **Lesson 3.2:** Solidity Fundamentals
  - Variables (state, local, global)
  - Data types (uint, address, bytes, string)
  - Functions and visibility modifiers
  - Events and logging
  - Control flow (if/else, loops)
  - Error handling (require, assert, revert)

- **Lesson 3.3:** Advanced Solidity
  - Structs for complex data
  - Arrays and mappings
  - Inheritance and interfaces
  - Libraries for code reuse
  - Gas optimization techniques

- **Lesson 3.4:** Testing & Deployment
  - Testing frameworks (Hardhat, Truffle, Foundry)
  - Writing unit tests
  - Deploying to testnets
  - Verifying contracts on Etherscan
  - Gas optimization

- **Lesson 3.5:** Project - ERC20 Token Contract
  - Implement ERC20 standard
  - Deploy to Goerli/Sepolia testnet
  - Write comprehensive tests
  - Verify on Etherscan
  - Full documentation

---

### **Module 4: Decentralized Finance (DeFi)** ✅
*5 Lessons | 55-90 minutes each*

- **Lesson 4.1:** DeFi Fundamentals
  - What is DeFi and its advantages
  - DeFi vs traditional finance
  - Smart contract protocols
  - Total Value Locked (TVL) metrics
  - Risk types in DeFi

- **Lesson 4.2:** Lending & Borrowing
  - Aave protocol mechanics
  - Compound lending pools
  - Interest rate models
  - Collateral and LTV ratios
  - Liquidation mechanisms
  - Flash loans

- **Lesson 4.3:** Automated Market Makers (AMMs)
  - Uniswap and other DEX
  - Constant product formula (xy=k)
  - Liquidity pools and LP tokens
  - Impermanent loss calculation
  - Concentrated liquidity (Uniswap v3)

- **Lesson 4.4:** Staking & Yield Farming
  - Validator staking mechanisms
  - Yield farming strategies
  - LP token rewards
  - Liquidity mining programs
  - APY calculations and comparison

- **Lesson 4.5:** Project - DeFi Strategy
  - Protocol research and selection
  - Risk assessment framework
  - Strategy execution
  - Return tracking
  - Emergency exit procedures

*5 Lessons | 50-90 minutes each*

- **Lesson 5.1:** Introduction to NFTs
  - What makes NFTs unique
  - ERC721 standard (unique tokens)
  - ERC1155 standard (multi-token)
  - Metadata and storage
  - Use cases (art, gaming, collectibles)

  - JSON metadata format
  - IPFS decentralized storage
  - Smart contract minting
  - Batch minting and gas optimization

- **Lesson 5.3:** NFT Marketplaces
  - OpenSea, Rarible, Blur platforms

  - Trading mechanics
  - Royalty configuration
  - Analytics and tracking

- **Lesson 5.4:** Legal & IP Considerations
  - Copyright and intellectual property
  - Licensing and permissions
  - Royalty standards (EIP-2981)
  - Legal frameworks by jurisdiction
  - Tax implications

- **Lesson 5.5:** Project - NFT Collection
  - Design and artwork creation
  - Metadata preparation
  - IPFS upload
  - Smart contract deployment
  - Marketplace launch
