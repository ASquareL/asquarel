Lesson 2.5: Project - Portfolio Management

Duration: 90 minutes | Difficulty: Intermediate

Project Overview

In this project, you'll create and manage a diversified cryptocurrency portfolio, track your cost basis, calculate returns, and understand tax implications.

Objective: Learn portfolio construction, tracking, and optimization strategies used by professional investors.

Project Steps

Step 1: Define Your Portfolio Strategy

  • Decide allocation strategy:
    • Core (60%): Bitcoin and Ethereum
    • Sector (30%): DeFi, Layer 2, Altcoins
    • Exploration (10%): New projects, high-risk
  • Create allocation spreadsheet
  • Document your investment thesis

Step 2: Research & Asset Selection

  • Research 5-10 cryptocurrencies:
    • Use CoinMarketCap or CoinGecko
    • Review fundamental metrics
    • Check team and technology
    • Assess use case and adoption
  • Write brief investment thesis for each
  • Calculate target position size

Step 3: Cost Basis Tracking

  • Create transaction log with:
    • Date and time of trade
    • Asset bought/sold
    • Quantity
    • Price per unit
    • Total cost (including fees)
    • Exchange/wallet
  • Calculate your average cost basis per asset
  • Total investment amount

Step 4: Performance Calculation

  • Calculate current portfolio value:
    • Current price × quantity per asset
    • Sum all positions
  • Calculate unrealized P&L:
    • Current value - Total cost = Unrealized P&L
    • Calculate as percentage
  • Track daily/weekly returns

Step 5: Diversification Analysis

  • Calculate allocation % per asset
  • Compare to target allocation
  • Identify rebalancing needs:
    • If Bitcoin < 50%, rebalance
    • If single altcoin > 20%, reduce
  • Document rebalancing decisions

Step 6: Risk Metrics

  • Calculate volatility:
    • Standard deviation of daily returns
    • Higher volatility = higher risk
  • Calculate max drawdown:
    • Largest % decline from peak
    • Shows worst-case scenario
  • Sharpe Ratio:
    • Return adjusted for risk
    • Higher is better

Step 7: Tax Implications

  • Research your jurisdiction's tax rules:
    • Capital gains tax (short-term vs long-term)
    • Income tax on staking rewards
    • Gift tax on transfers
  • Calculate tax liability:
    • Realized gains × tax rate
    • Identify tax-loss harvesting opportunities
  • Document for accountant

Step 8: Portfolio Dashboard

  • Create dashboard showing:
    • Current portfolio value
    • Total invested
    • Unrealized P&L ($and %)
    • Allocation breakdown
    • Top and worst performers
    • Asset allocation chart
  • Update daily/weekly

Portfolio Tools

  • CoinGecko API: Free cryptocurrency data
  • Delta App: Portfolio tracker
  • Zapper: DeFi portfolio tracker
  • Google Sheets: DIY tracking (recommended for learning)

Professional Portfolio Rules

The 60/30/10 Rule

  • 60% Core holdings (stable, proven assets)
  • 30% Sector bets (DeFi, NFT, Layer 2, etc.)
  • 10% Exploration (new, high-risk projects)

Position Sizing

  • No single position > 20% of portfolio
  • Risk per position ≤ 2% of total capital
  • Allocate based on conviction level

Rebalancing

  • Rebalance quarterly or when allocations drift >10%
  • Buy underweighted, sell outperformers
  • Maintains risk profile

Common Portfolio Mistakes

  • Not diversifying (single asset risk)
  • Not tracking cost basis
  • Letting winners run too large
  • Emotional panic selling
  • Ignoring tax implications
  • Not documenting transactions

Deliverables

Submit documentation including:

  • Investment thesis and strategy
  • Cost basis spreadsheet
  • Performance analysis
  • Risk metrics
  • Tax analysis
  • Portfolio dashboard
  • Rebalancing recommendations
Remember: Past performance doesn't guarantee future results. Diversification reduces risk but doesn't eliminate it. Always do your own research.
