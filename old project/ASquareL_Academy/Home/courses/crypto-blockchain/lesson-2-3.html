Lesson 2.3: Trading Fundamentals

Duration: 60 minutes | Difficulty: Intermediate

Basic Trading Concepts

Trading in crypto can be exciting but risky. Understanding fundamental concepts is essential before risking your money.

Bid and Ask Prices

  • Bid: The price someone is willing to pay
  • Ask: The price someone is willing to sell at
  • Spread: Difference between bid and ask
  • You always lose the spread when trading

Market Liquidity

  • High Liquidity: Many buyers and sellers, tight spreads
  • Low Liquidity: Few buyers/sellers, wide spreads
  • Bitcoin has the highest liquidity
  • Altcoins may have much wider spreads

Long vs Short Positions

Long Position (Going Long)

  • Buy a cryptocurrency expecting price to increase
  • Profit if price goes up
  • Loss if price goes down
  • Simplest way to trade
  • Available on most exchanges

Short Position (Going Short)

  • Borrow cryptocurrency to sell it
  • Expect price to decrease
  • Buy it back at lower price and return borrowed amount
  • Profit on the difference
  • Risky if price increases
  • Requires margin account and paying borrowing fees
Caution: Shorting is advanced trading with unlimited downside risk if the price rises. Never short without stop-losses.

Position Sizing & Risk Management

Risk Management Rules

  • Risk Only What You Can Lose: Never trade with money you need
  • Position Sizing: Risk only 1-2% of portfolio per trade
  • Use Stop-Losses: Always protect your downside
  • Take Profits: Don't be greedy
  • Diversify: Don't put all money in one trade

Example Position Sizing

  • Account: $10,000
  • Risk per trade: 1% = $100
  • If you buy at $50 with $100 risk, your stop-loss is $45
  • This determines your position size

Entry and Exit Strategies

Entry Points

  • Market Order Entry: Buy immediately
  • Limit Order Entry: Buy at specific price
  • Dollar Cost Averaging: Buy fixed amount over time
  • Technical Entry: Based on chart analysis

Exit Strategies

  • Take Profit Target: Set a profit goal upfront
  • Stop-Loss: Cut losses if wrong
  • Trailing Stop: Stops rise with price, locks in gains
  • Time-Based: Exit after set period
Golden Rule: Decide your exit BEFORE you enter. Plan both your profit target and stop-loss level beforehand.

Common Trading Mistakes

Emotional Trading

  • Trading based on fear or greed
  • FOMO (Fear of Missing Out)
  • Panic selling during dips
  • Revenge trading after losses

Technical Mistakes

  • Not using stop-losses
  • Over-leveraging (risking more than you have)
  • Trading low-liquidity altcoins
  • Holding through obvious support breaks

How to Avoid Mistakes

  • Have a written trading plan
  • Use automatic stop-losses
  • Trade only with money you can afford to lose
  • Keep emotions out of it
  • Learn from losses, don't chase them

Types of Market Orders

Market Order

Execute immediately at current market price. Used when speed matters more than price precision.

Limit Order

Set maximum buy or minimum sell price. Executes only if price reaches your limit. Gives price control but may not fill.

Stop-Loss Order

Automatically sell if price drops to trigger level. Protects against catastrophic losses.

Take-Profit Order

Automatically sell if price rises to target. Locks in gains without needing to monitor.

Leverage Trading (Advanced)

Borrowing money to trade larger amounts. Amplifies both gains and losses.

Leverage Basics

  • 2x Leverage: Trade with 2x your capital
  • 10x Leverage: Trade with 10x your capital
  • Interest paid on borrowed amount
  • Liquidation risk if position moves against you
High Risk: Leverage trading has wiped out many traders. Beginners should NEVER use leverage until they have experience.

Trading Fees & Costs

  • Trading Fee: 0.1-0.25% per trade (CEX typical)
  • Spread Cost: Difference between bid and ask
  • Network Fees: Blockchain transaction costs
  • Slippage: Price movement between order and execution

Fee Impact Example

  • Buy $1,000 Bitcoin at 0.1% fee = $1 cost
  • Sell at 0.1% fee = $1 cost
  • Total round-trip cost: $2
  • Need 0.2% price gain just to break even

Your First Trade Checklist

  1. Choose a reputable exchange
  2. Complete identity verification
  3. Deposit funds (start small)
  4. Practice with limit orders first
  5. Set stop-loss BEFORE entering
  6. Trade small amounts while learning
  7. Never use leverage as beginner
  8. Withdraw profits to cold wallet regularly
