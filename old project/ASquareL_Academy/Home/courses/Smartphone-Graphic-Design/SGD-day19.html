DAY 19: HIGH-CONVERTING MARKETING FLYERS

Design commercially viable flyers with proven conversion principles

Sales-Driving Design

Flyers aren't art—they're sales tools. Every design decision serves conversion. Color psychology, typography hierarchy, white space, call-to-action placement—all are strategic weapons in your conversion arsenal.

The Flyer Formula

Headline: Attention-grabbing benefit. Large, bold, compelling. Visuals: Professional photography or illustration. Emotion-driving. Body Copy: Benefit-focused, not feature-focused. Why should I care? CTA: Clear, contrasting, clickable (or scannable). Trust Elements: Logo, testimonials, credentials.

CONVERSION RULE: Every element serves the call-to-action. If something doesn't push toward CTA, it's noise. Remove it. Ruthlessly prioritize conversion.

Visual Hierarchy in Flyers

Viewer's eye travels: headline first, then image, then body, then CTA. Structure design following this path. Make headline impossible to miss. Make CTA impossible to ignore. Everything else supports this journey.

Mobile Flyer Optimization

Flyers are shared digitally now. Optimize for phone viewing. Ensure text is readable at small sizes. High contrast. Large touch-friendly buttons. Responsive design thinking applies to flyers too.

Color and Psychology in Sales

Red urgency buttons increase clicks. Blue conveys trust. Green conveys growth. Yellow catches attention. Choose colors matching conversion goals. Red flyers outsell blue for limited-time offers. Blue flyers outsell red for premium services. Psychology matters.

CONVERSION TIP: A/B test colors with actual audience. What converts for tech doesn't convert for hospitality. Know your audience. Design for them, not for yourself.
