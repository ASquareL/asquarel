Module 6: Security & Best Practices

Lesson 6.1: Crypto Security Essentials

  • Hardware wallet setup and maintenance
  • Seed phrase management and backup
  • Avoiding phishing and social engineering
  • Device security practices
  • Multi-signature wallets
Critical: Your private keys are your entire financial life in crypto. Protect them like passwords to your bank account combined with the keys to your house.

Lesson 6.2: Smart Contract Audits

  • Reading and understanding audit reports
  • Critical vs medium vs low severity findings
  • Verified contracts on Etherscan
  • Code review methodology
  • Common vulnerability patterns

Lesson 6.3: Attack Vectors & Defense

  • Reentrancy attacks and prevention (Checks-Effects-Interactions)
  • Integer overflow/underflow fixes (SafeMath, Solidity 0.8+)
  • Front-running and MEV (Miner Extractable Value)
  • Oracle manipulation attacks
  • Flash loan attacks
Prevention is Key: Most hacks could have been prevented with proper code review and testing. Never deploy untested contracts to mainnet.

Lesson 6.4: DAO Governance

  • What are DAOs (Decentralized Autonomous Organizations)
  • Governance tokens and voting power
  • Proposal mechanisms
  • Voting participation and delegation
  • Treasury management

Lesson 6.5: Project - Security Audit

Conduct a thorough security review including:

  • Code walkthrough and analysis
  • Vulnerability identification
  • Testing and verification
  • Formal audit report
  • Recommendations for remediation
