Lesson 2.4: Market Analysis

Duration: 60 minutes | Difficulty: Intermediate

Technical Analysis Basics

Technical analysis studies historical price and volume data to forecast future price movement. It's based on the idea that price trends repeat.

Candlestick Charts

  • Body: Open to close price
  • Wick (Shadow): High and low during period
  • Green: Close higher than open (bullish)
  • Red: Close lower than open (bearish)
  • Each candle represents a time period (1min, 5min, 1hr, 1day)

Chart Timeframes

  • Scalping: 1-5 minute charts
  • Day Trading: 15-60 minute charts
  • Swing Trading: 4-hour to daily charts
  • Position Trading: Weekly or monthly charts

Key Technical Indicators

Moving Averages (MA)

  • Simple MA: Average of prices over set period
  • Exponential MA: Weights recent prices more heavily
  • Use: Identify trend direction
  • Golden Cross: When fast MA crosses above slow MA (bullish)

RSI (Relative Strength Index)

  • Measures momentum on scale of 0-100
  • <30: Oversold (potential bounce)
  • >70: Overbought (potential pullback)
  • Good for identifying extremes

MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)

  • Trend following momentum indicator
  • Shows relationship between moving averages
  • Signal Line Crossover: Common trading signal
  • Good for trend confirmation

Bollinger Bands

  • Shows support and resistance zones
  • Upper and lower bands indicate extremes
  • Squeeze indicates low volatility (breakout coming)
  • Price outside bands is extreme

Volume Analysis

  • High Volume: Strong move confirmation
  • Low Volume: Weak move, low confidence
  • Volume Increase: Trend strengthening
  • Volume Decrease: Trend weakening

Support & Resistance

Support Levels

  • Price levels where buying pressure appears
  • Prices tend to bounce up at support
  • Breakthrough below support is bearish signal
  • Often at previous lows or round numbers

Resistance Levels

  • Price levels where selling pressure appears
  • Prices tend to bounce down at resistance
  • Breakthrough above resistance is bullish signal
  • Often at previous highs or round numbers

Dynamic Support/Resistance

  • Moving average acts as dynamic support/resistance
  • Price bouncing on MA shows trend strength
  • Break through MA indicates trend reversal

Chart Patterns

Bullish Patterns

  • Double Bottom: V-shape recovery
  • Triangle: Breakout to upside likely
  • Flag: Consolidation before continued rise

Bearish Patterns

  • Double Top: Inverted V, rejection of highs
  • Head and Shoulders: Three peaks, middle higher
  • Descending Triangle: Breakout downward likely
Pattern Recognition: Patterns give probability edges, not certainties. Always use stop-losses with pattern trades.

Fundamental Analysis

Fundamental analysis evaluates the intrinsic value and adoption potential of a cryptocurrency.

Key Metrics

  • Market Cap: Price × circulating supply
  • Volume: Trading activity and liquidity
  • Active Addresses: Number of active accounts
  • Transaction Count: Network usage
  • Developer Activity: GitHub commits and updates

What to Research

  • Whitepaper and technology
  • Team experience and credibility
  • Use case and real-world adoption
  • Competition and market positioning
  • Regulatory environment
  • Community and social metrics

On-Chain Analysis

Analyzing blockchain data to understand investor behavior.

Key On-Chain Metrics

  • Exchange Flows: Coins moving in/out of exchanges
  • Whale Activity: Large holder movements
  • HODL Waves: Age of coins being moved
  • Network Value: Total economic value secured

Risk Assessment

Evaluate Before Trading

  • Market cap (avoid extremely low cap coins)
  • Liquidity (can you exit when needed?)
  • Volatility (can you handle price swings?)
  • Regulatory risks (could be delisted?)
  • Technology risks (code audits, security)

Trading Plan Template

  1. Setup: What conditions trigger a trade?
  2. Entry: Exact price and order type
  3. Stop-Loss: Where you exit if wrong
  4. Take-Profit: Target profit level
  5. Risk/Reward: Calculate ratio before entering
Technical vs Fundamental: Technical analysis is for timing, fundamental analysis is for selection. Use both together for best results.
