DAY 20: PHOTO MANIPULATION TECHNIQUES

Transform raw photography into design assets with professional finesse

Layers and Non-Destructive Editing

Always work non-destructively. Use adjustment layers. Keep original photo untouched. This allows infinite revisions without quality loss. Professional photography workflows depend on this discipline.

Essential Photo Adjustments

Exposure: Brighten dark photos, darken blown-out photos. Saturation: Intensify or mute colors. Contrast: Add punch or soften mood. Temperature: Warm or cool tone. Vibrance: Selective color intensity. Master these fundamentals. Most photo issues resolve through these five adjustments.

PHOTO TIP: Shoot your own photos when possible. Professional photography is expensive. But mediocre photos hurt design more than good design helps bad photos.

Blending and Compositing

Blend multiple photos into one seamless image. Use layer masks. Feather edges. Create smooth transitions. Compositing transforms photography into artistic interpretation. This skill multiplies your design possibilities infinitely.

Color Grading

Apply color filters creating consistent mood across multiple photos. Warm tones suggest comfort, nostalgia. Cool tones suggest technology, professionalism. Consistent color grading across a campaign creates cohesion.

Smart Object Workflows

Smart objects preserve original photo quality through transformations. Scale, rotate, warp—smart objects maintain editability. This enables endless revision without quality degradation. Professional designers rely on smart objects.

MANIPULATION TIP: The best photo manipulation is invisible. Viewers shouldn't notice manipulation—they should only notice beauty. Subtlety is mastery.
