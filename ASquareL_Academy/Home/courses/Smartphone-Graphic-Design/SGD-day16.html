DAY 16: SHAPE MANIPULATION & GEOMETRY

Command geometric principles and transform basic shapes into sophisticated elements

Geometric Fundamentals

Circles: Perfect, infinite movement, complete. Squares: Stability, order, control. Triangles: Dynamic, direction, tension. Hexagons: Harmony, nature, balance. Understand geometric psychology. Choose shapes matching design intent.

Boolean Operations

Union: Combine shapes. Subtract: Remove one shape from another. Intersect: Keep only overlapping area. Divide: Create separate paths from intersection. These operations multiply shape possibilities infinitely.

BOOLEAN RULE: Master boolean operations. They're the bridge between simple shapes and complex designs. One rectangle + subtract = infinite possibilities.

Transformation Techniques

Rotation: Rotate shapes to create dynamic angles. 45-degree rotation breaks static feeling. Scaling: Proportional or non-proportional. Both create meaning. Skewing: Create perspective and motion. Mirroring: Create perfect symmetry or intentional asymmetry.

Bezier Curves

Bezier paths bend geometry into organic forms. Control handles determine curve tension. Master bezier curves and you can create any shape. This is cornerstone of vector design mastery.

Compound Shapes

Complex design emerges from simple shape combinations. A leaf: ellipse rotated and skewed. A star: triangles arranged radially. A flower: circles scaled and rotated. Think in component shapes. Build complexity from simplicity.

SHAPE TIP: When stuck on design, return to basic shapes. Rethink in circles and rectangles. Find the essential shape underneath. Everything complex builds from simplicity.
