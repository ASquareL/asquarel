DAY 8: FONT PAIRING METHODOLOGY

Discover the science behind harmonious font combinations

The Pairing Principle

Never use a single font for everything. Pairing creates contrast and visual interest. But pairing poorly creates visual chaos. The rule: Pair serif with sans-serif. Or pair different sans-serifs with contrasting weights.

Font Families

Serif: Timeless, elegant, traditional. Sans-serif: Modern, clean, contemporary. Monospace: Technical, code-oriented. Script: Decorative, celebratory. Choose families that serve your content.

PAIRING RULE: If two fonts look too similar, they clash. They fight for attention without creating intended contrast. Make the difference obvious. Bold vs. thin. Serif vs. sans. Let contrasts sing.

High Contrast Pairings

Display serif paired with body sans-serif. Example: Playfair Display + Inter. This pairing gives elegance + readability. Classic combination trusted by professional designers for years.

Modern Pairings

Two sans-serifs work when weights differ dramatically. Example: Oswald (bold) + Open Sans (regular). The weight difference creates visual hierarchy without font conflict.

Mobile Font Selection

Small screens demand readable fonts. Avoid thin weights at body sizes. Avoid decorative scripts for body text. Headlines can be adventurous. Body text must be reader-friendly. Test all font choices at small scales.

MOBILE FONT TIP: Test your font at 14px on actual phones. Squint. Can you read it comfortably? If not, choose different font. Small screens leave no room for beautiful-but-unreadable choices.

System Fonts vs. Custom Fonts

System fonts (San Francisco, Roboto) load instantly. Custom fonts take time. On mobile, seconds matter. When in doubt, use system fonts. They're designed for device resolution. They're proven. They work.

When to Use Custom Fonts

Brand-defining moments. Logos. Headlines that need personality. Not every pixel needs personality. Use custom fonts strategically. Sprinkle them like seasoning, not flour.
