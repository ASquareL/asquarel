DAY 22: PRINT-READY OPTIMIZATION

Prepare designs for professional printing with CMYK workflows and precision specifications

RGB vs. CMYK Color Spaces

RGB: Screen colors (light-based). Additive. Wide gamut. CMYK: Print colors (ink-based). Subtractive. Limited gamut. Design in RGB, convert to CMYK for print. Many colors look different in print. Test conversions before printing.

CMYK Conversion Strategy

Convert early. Design viewing your CMYK preview, not RGB. This prevents shocking surprises at print time. Some RGB colors cannot print. Know this. Plan accordingly. Desaturate colors 10-15% as safety margin.

COLOR RULE: Never print RGB files. Always convert to CMYK. Always embed color profile. Always test proofs. Print surprises are expensive surprises.

Resolution and Bleed Settings

Resolution: 300 DPI minimum for print. 600 DPI for high-quality. Bleed: Add 0.125" bleed extending past trim. This prevents white lines at cut edges. Margins: Keep important content 0.5" inside trim. Prevents critical content cutoff.

File Preparation

Flatten layers for printing. Convert all fonts to outlines (prevent font substitution). Embed all images (prevent missing links). Save as PDF for print. PDF ensures consistency across systems.

Print Provider Communication

Know your printer's specifications BEFORE designing. Paper type, finish, capabilities—all affect final output. Communicate with printer. Provide specifications. Ask for proofs. Printer expertise prevents disasters.

PRINT TIP: Request physical proof before full print run. Screen colors deceive. Physical proofs reveal reality. Budget for proofs. They're cheap insurance.
