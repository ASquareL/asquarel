Our Story

From a simple idea to a connected ecosystem.

How A Square L Innovate grew from a single conviction into three integrated capabilities.

A Square L Innovate was founded in 2023 with a clear conviction: the modern growth equation only works when talent, technology, and infrastructure reinforce each other — not when they live in separate silos.

We built three connected capabilities: a training ground for future software engineers, a client-facing software delivery arm, and an operational backbone that supports the business systems modern enterprises rely on.

Each division strengthens the others. Learners get real project experience. Clients get software built by a trained, aligned team. Businesses get operational support grounded in the realities of running at scale.

We didn't set out to build three businesses. We set out to build one ecosystem — where everything reinforces everything else. That's the real leverage.

— Ashiru Auwal Lawal, Founder
