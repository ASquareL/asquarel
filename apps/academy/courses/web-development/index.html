Frequently Asked Questions

Is this course really beginner-friendly?

Yes. We start from zero. Module 1 assumes no prior experience — the first lesson explains what a web page is, and by lesson 8 you've built one yourself.

How long does it take to finish?

At 30 minutes a day, roughly 3 months. At 1 hour a day, about 6 weeks. There's no deadline — go at the pace that fits your life.

Can I learn from my phone?

Yes. Every lesson is designed to work on a phone. For coding practice, a laptop makes things easier — but it isn't required to start or complete the material.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Complete all 52 lessons and you earn a certificate you can share on LinkedIn, attach to job applications, or add to your portfolio.

What if I get stuck?

Every module has discussion threads. Pro and Mentorship plan students get direct access to the mentor. Free plan students have community support.

Will this get me a job?

It gives you the foundation, real projects for your portfolio, and the skills employers actually ask for. Job outcomes depend on how much you practice and build beyond the course.
