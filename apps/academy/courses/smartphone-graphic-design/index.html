Frequently Asked Questions

Which phone do I need?

Any modern Android or iPhone works. We recommend free apps that run on both — you don''t need to buy anything.

Do I need design experience?

No. We start with the absolute basics — no prior design knowledge required. Just curiosity and willingness to practice.

Can I really design professionally on a phone?

Yes. Thousands of Nigerian designers do it every day. This course teaches the same tools and principles they use.

How long does it take?

At 30 minutes a day, roughly 8 weeks. At 1 hour a day, about 5 weeks. No deadlines — go at your own pace.

Will this help me get clients?

It gives you the skills and portfolio pieces. Client acquisition is a separate business skill — but this is the foundation.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Complete all 40 lessons and you earn a shareable certificate you can attach to portfolio or job applications.
