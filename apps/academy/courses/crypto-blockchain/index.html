Frequently Asked Questions

Is crypto safe to learn?

Yes, when you follow best practices. We teach safety-first from lesson one — you''ll practice on test networks before ever touching real funds.

Do I need money to start?

No. You can complete the entire course on test networks. Real funds are only needed when you''re ready to actually use what you''ve learned.

Is this financial advice?

No. This is education. We teach you how the technology works — you decide what to do with that knowledge.

What if I lose my wallet access?

We cover recovery extensively. By the end of Module 3, you''ll have a bulletproof backup system and know exactly how to recover your funds.

Which blockchain do we focus on?

Primarily Bitcoin and Ethereum, since they''re the foundations. We also cover layer-2s and popular alternatives where relevant.

Will this get me rich?

No promises. This course teaches you how the technology works. Wealth depends on your decisions, discipline, and risk management — none of which we can guarantee.
