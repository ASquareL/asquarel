Common Questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

Six answers to the most common enrollment questions.

Is the Academy really free to start?

Yes. You can create a free account and access the starter lessons and free plan courses without paying anything. Pro plans unlock everything.

How much time do I need each week?

Most students commit 5–10 hours per week. The material is self-paced — you go as fast or as slow as your schedule allows.

Do I need a laptop or can I use my phone?

Both work. Every lesson is designed to be usable on a phone. For coding tracks, a laptop makes things easier — but isn't required to start.

Do I get a certificate when I finish?

Yes. Complete any track and earn a certificate you can share on LinkedIn, attach to job applications, or add to your portfolio.

How does mentorship work?

Pro and Mentorship plan students get direct access to Ashiru and the team. Free plan students have community support and self-paced materials.

Can I switch tracks later?

Absolutely. Enroll in as many tracks as you want. Many students start with Web Development and later add Crypto or Design.
